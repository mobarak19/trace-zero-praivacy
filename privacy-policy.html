    <section>

        <h2>1. Overview</h2>


        <p>

            Trace Zero is a privacy-focused media utility designed to help you

            remove supported metadata from photos, videos, and audio files and,

            where available, redact visual information such as faces, license

            plates, and documents.

        </p>


        <p>

            We designed Trace Zero around an on-device processing model.

            Your selected media is intended to remain on your device while

            Trace Zero performs its cleaning or redaction operations.

        </p>


        <div class="highlight">

            <strong>Privacy by design:</strong>

            Trace Zero does not require you to upload your personal photos,

            videos, or audio files to a Trace Zero server to perform its

            core cleaning and redaction features.

        </div>

    </section>


    <section>

        <h2>2. Information We Process</h2>


        <p>

            Trace Zero may access information contained within media that you

            explicitly select for processing.

        </p>


        <h3>Photos</h3>

        <p>

            Depending on your selected privacy settings, Trace Zero may process

            supported photo metadata such as GPS/location information, dates,

            device information, EXIF information, and other supported metadata.

        </p>


        <h3>Videos</h3>

        <p>

            Depending on your selected settings, Trace Zero may process supported

            video metadata including location, date, device information,

            software information, descriptions, and other supported metadata.

        </p>


        <h3>Audio</h3>

        <p>

            Depending on your selected settings, Trace Zero may process supported

            audio metadata such as metadata, location, date, and embedded artwork.

        </p>


        <h3>Redaction</h3>

        <p>

            When you use a redaction feature, Trace Zero may analyze the selected

            media locally to identify supported visual regions such as faces,

            license plates, documents, or other supported areas that you choose

            to redact.

        </p>

    </section>


    <section>

        <h2>3. Photos and Media Library Access</h2>


        <p>

            Trace Zero requests access to your Photos library only when necessary

            for a feature you initiate, such as selecting a photo or video for

            cleaning or redaction.

        </p>


        <p>

            Trace Zero does not automatically scan your entire Photos library.

            You control which media you provide to the app through Apple's

            system-provided media selection interfaces.

        </p>


        <p>

            If selected media is stored in iCloud and is not currently available

            locally, Apple's Photos framework may need to download the selected

            original before Trace Zero can process it.

        </p>

    </section>


    <section>

        <h2>4. On-Device Processing</h2>


        <p>

            Trace Zero's core cleaning and redaction workflows are designed to

            process selected media locally on your device.

        </p>


        <p>

            This means that your selected media is not intentionally uploaded

            to Trace Zero's servers as part of the normal metadata-cleaning or

            redaction workflow.

        </p>


        <p>

            Processing may create temporary files or cleaned copies locally

            on your device. These files are used to complete the requested

            operation and may be removed according to the app's implementation

            and operating-system storage behavior.

        </p>

    </section>


    <section>

        <h2>5. iCloud</h2>


        <p>

            Trace Zero may interact with Apple's Photos framework when you

            select media from your Photos library.

        </p>


        <p>

            If Apple needs to download an iCloud-stored original to your device,

            that transfer is handled by Apple's systems and is subject to Apple's

            own terms and privacy practices.

        </p>


        <p>

            Trace Zero does not operate Apple's iCloud service.

        </p>

    </section>


    <section>

        <h2>6. Redaction and Computer Vision</h2>


        <p>

            Trace Zero may use on-device computer vision technologies to identify

            supported visual content for redaction.

        </p>


        <p>

            Depending on the feature, this may include detection of faces,

            license plates, documents, or other supported visual regions.

        </p>


        <p>

            Detection systems are not guaranteed to identify every object,

            person, plate, document, or sensitive area. You are responsible for

            reviewing the result before sharing or publishing the processed media.

        </p>

    </section>

            <li>Your videos</li>

            <li>Your audio files</li>

            <li>The original media files you select</li>

            <li>The cleaned media files produced by the app</li>

            <li>GPS coordinates contained in your media</li>

            <li>Photo or video metadata for advertising purposes</li>

        </ul>


        <p>

            This list describes the intended behavior of the core application.

            Platform services, purchases, crash reporting, analytics, or other
